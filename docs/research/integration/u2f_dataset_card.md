## Intended use

This universe supports controlled research on whether a system should read longitudinal patient-state fixtures, retrieve external-evidence fixtures, combine capabilities, or abstain. It also supports replay-contract tests for temporal visibility, evidence lineage, and deterministic action-arm comparisons. It is not clinical data and is not suitable for clinical advice, diagnosis, treatment, real-patient simulation claims, or medical decision support.

U2-F does not train or evaluate a learned model. If all gates pass, only partial policy labels for memory_read, external_retrieval, and answerability are qualified for a separately authorized training stage. This does not qualify architecture selection, budgets, team composition, full execution policy, or post-training.

## Composition

The universe contains synthetic subject timelines with 80 records over 729 days, five record types, four history regimes, 14 TRAIN task families, three synthetic evidence-family labels, four reserved structural-DEV template families, and six answer types. Subjects, seeds, counterfactual siblings, and structural templates are isolated by split. A later report records the actual distributions and shortcut/control metrics.

All query values, keys, dates, names, and evidence records are generated by repository-owned deterministic grammars. No benchmark examples, third-party medical document text, WHO/CDC document text, real patient data, or PHI are copied into the dataset. The source-independence audit scans the generated output for reserved benchmark/source identifiers and records its complete scan result.

## Files and trust boundaries

- `train/episodes.jsonl`, `dev_iid/episodes.jsonl`, `dev_structural/episodes.jsonl`: runtime-only inputs.
- Per-split `evaluator_truth.jsonl`: answer keys, dependency requirements, and evaluation truth; never exposed to runtime.
- Per-split `partial_policy_supervision.jsonl`: the limited capability/answerability labels and their eligibility status.
- Per-split and root `lineage.jsonl`: generation provenance.
- Root `latent_worlds.jsonl` and `natural_language_realizations.jsonl`: owned source/replay views.
- Audit JSON files and `report.md`: distribution, split, temporal, shortcut, counterfactual, source-independence, and integrity results.
- `reserved_test_plan.json`: reserved membership plan only; contains no generated rows.

The generation audit verifies physical separation between runtime and evaluator files, validates lineage on all 5,120 active episodes, and keeps architecture and budget fields unresolved. The dataset is not a blind external benchmark; DEV is project-owned and intended for internal development after its membership and access policy are respected.

## Limitations

This is a synthetic contract environment. Its deterministic source records, fixed tool surface, generated English wording, synthetic patient-state store, and synthetic retrieval families do not model the full ambiguity or distribution of real clinical records or medical literature. A passing synthetic audit establishes generator consistency and the declared evaluation boundary only. It does not establish clinical validity, external-source quality, or model performance.

## Access, redistribution, and governance

The active rows are project-authored synthetic artifacts. External benchmarks and external medical documents are not embedded in these files. Keep generated notices and provenance fields intact when copying or reusing the data. Do not claim any external source-family label represents a licensed or reviewed production corpus. Follow the repository's separate U2-D source registry for any future external benchmark or production retrieval use.
