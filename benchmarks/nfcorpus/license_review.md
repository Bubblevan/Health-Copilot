# NFCorpus license and admissibility review

Status: `REVIEW_REQUIRED`

The BEIR package/release identity and the upstream NFCorpus use conditions are
recorded separately. A public download URL is not treated as a redistribution
grant. Before E1, a human reviewer must confirm the upstream license, citation
requirements and whether a normalized derived artifact may be committed.

Raw external data is not committed to Git. Until review and a SHA-256 pin are
recorded, NFCorpus cannot be marked `APPROVED`.
